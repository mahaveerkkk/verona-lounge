The Verona Philosophy

Where Ancient Fire Meets
Modern Botanical Mastery

At Verona, dining is not merely a meal—it is a nocturnal sensory journey. Our kitchen revolves around an authentic imported volcanic stone woodfire oven, blistering hand-tossed Neapolitan dough and searing prime cuts at 480°C.

Every herb is clipped fresh from our rooftop botanical garden, and every cocktail is clarified, smoked with applewood, or infused in-house for a taste that lingers long after midnight.
